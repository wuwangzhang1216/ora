Your speech.
Your choice.

Ora processes speech on your Mac by default. External AI backends are optional.

Updated November 14, 2026 · Applies to Ora 0.10.1 and later. Earlier versions may not show the per-session AI sharing confirmation.

Local recognition and translation

With Local speech recognition and the MLX Swift translation backend, audio, recognized text, and translations are processed on your Mac. Ora does not upload this content to the developer. Audio is held in memory for processing; Ora does not save raw audio recordings.

Model downloads

First use and changes to model quality may download model weights and configuration files from Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure. These requests reveal ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and the requested model, to the hosting service. They do not include your speech or transcript. Once complete model files are cached, the default local backends can work offline. Optional Apple recognition downloads language assets through macOS.

Optional AI services

Before each listening session using RealRouter or Rapid-MLX, Ora identifies the configured server and asks for permission to send data. RealRouter speech recognition sends audio segments. API translation sends recognized text, the target language, and recent source/translation context from the current session. This can include other speakers captured by your selected audio input. The configured server may forward requests to its upstream AI providers.

Rapid-MLX normally runs on your own Mac, but its URL is configurable. A remote server receives the same translation input. Ora does not control the privacy, retention, training, or deletion practices of servers you configure. Review those providers’ policies before allowing access. Cancel the prompt or choose local backends to avoid sharing speech with an external service. Stopping listening ends the session permission.

“Save & Test APIs” asks separately before sending a synthetic English sample. It does not record your microphone or upload transcript history. API keys are stored in macOS Keychain and sent to your configured API server to authenticate requests.

History, exports, and deletion

Recognized source text and completed translations are saved locally in Ora’s application data folder until you delete them. Open Preferences → History to manage or delete history. Exported files are saved at the location you choose; delete those copies separately. Ora does not automatically upload your transcript history. Your own device backup or file syncing settings may include local data or exported files.

Tracking and accounts

Ora includes no advertising or analytics SDK and requires no Ora account. Ora does not sell your speech or use it to train a developer-operated model. App Store transactions, operating system diagnostics, and optional service accounts are governed by the respective providers’ policies.

Permissions and control

Microphone input requires macOS microphone permission. System audio requires Screen & System Audio Recording permission; Ora consumes audio and does not save screen images. Listening is indicated in the caption window and menu bar. Stop listening at any time, and revoke permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Ora project support channel. GitHub issues are public: do not post confidential transcripts, recordings, API keys, or other private information. Changes to this policy are published on this page.

中文说明

默认的本地语音识别和 MLX Swift 翻译均在 Mac 上运行，不向开发者上传语音或字幕，也不保存原始录音。首次使用需要下载模型；完整缓存后，默认本地模型可离线运行。

RealRouter 和 Rapid-MLX 是可选服务。每次使用这些服务开始监听前，Ora 会显示服务器地址并询问是否允许发送数据。API 识别会发送音频片段；API 翻译会发送识别文字、目标语言及当前会话的近期翻译上下文，其中可能包含其他人的发言。服务商及其上游模型的保留、训练和删除政策由其自行规定。停止监听即结束本次授权；选择本地识别与 MLX Swift 即可避免向外部服务发送语音内容。

字幕历史保存在本机，可在设置的 History 页面删除；导出的副本需要单独删除。API 密钥保存在 macOS 钥匙串。Ora 无广告或分析 SDK，无需 Ora 账号。隐私问题可通过项目支持渠道联系；公开问题区请勿提交机密录音、字幕或密钥。
