A little help
getting started.

Ora lives in your Mac’s menu bar. Click the circular Ora icon to open its controls.

Requirements

An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 15 or later. First use needs an internet connection and several gigabytes of free storage for local models. Larger quality settings require more memory and storage. Apple’s optional streaming recognizer requires macOS 26 or later and currently uses English input.

Start translating

  1. Open Ora, then click its circular menu bar icon and choose Preferences.
  2. Choose Microphone, System Audio, or Meeting as your audio source. Choose your target language.
  3. For local processing, select Local (Qwen ASR) and MLX Swift. Wait for the models to finish preparing.
  4. Choose Start Listening. Allow the relevant macOS permission when requested. Play speech or speak into your microphone.
  5. If the caption window is hidden, choose Show Caption Window in the Ora menu. Use Caption Layout to choose bilingual, translation-only, or compact captions.

No audio or captions?

Check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording. Quit and reopen Ora after changing permissions. A separate test build may need its own permission. On Mac notebooks, the built-in microphone is unavailable with the lid closed; use an external microphone or open the lid.

Slow or inaccurate translations?

Translation speed depends on model size, your Mac, and the audio workload. Try Standard for lower memory usage or High for a larger local model. Keep other heavy workloads low. Generated captions can contain errors in names, numbers, negation, and currency; check critical details against the original speech. Ora does not guarantee a fixed latency or professional interpretation accuracy.

History and exports

Choose Transcript History from the Ora menu to review saved text. Export a session or all history to a folder you select. Manage deletion in Preferences → History. The app saves text, not raw audio recordings.

Report a problem

Open an Ora support issue with your Ora version, macOS version, Mac model, selected audio source and backends, and a short description. GitHub issues are public. Remove private transcripts, recordings, keys, and identifying details from screenshots or logs.

Read the privacy policy before enabling an external API service.
